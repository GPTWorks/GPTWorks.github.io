The pack, in order

  1. Read the basics

    Use SC Tenant Help for official links and a plain starting point. Then open the Landlord & Tenant Act reader.

  2. Save what happened

    Keep dates, texts, photos, and repair requests. If you later score a landlord, stick to how they treated you — not the apartment finishes.

  3. Only after you move out: score them

    Score My Landlord is for past landlords. A current review can put your housing at risk.

  4. Go deeper if you need to

    SC Civil Rules covers procedures and forms. TenantGPT is there when you want to ask in plain language.

Past landlords only for scoring. Do not score a landlord you still rent from. Wait until you have moved out, then leave a review by the property address you rented.

Quick checklist

  • Something broken? Put the request in writing and keep a copy.
  • Deposit dispute? Note move-out date, forwarding address, and photos.
  • Notice or eviction paperwork? Read it carefully, then check the Act and civil rules — or get legal help.
  • Ready to help others? After you move out, score that past landlord.

This pack is information, not legal advice. For personal legal help, contact South Carolina Legal Services or a licensed attorney.
