Before you start

Past landlords only. Do not score a landlord you still rent from. A current review can put your housing at risk. Wait until you have moved out.

  • You only need the address of the place you rented and your experience with that landlord.
  • Right now, scores are posted publicly for Greenville County, SC properties.
  • If your address is elsewhere in South Carolina, still submit. We keep the review and will post that county when enough reviews arrive.
  • Anyone can submit. We store reviews in our database. We do not share your information with anyone, and we never contact a landlord because of a review.
