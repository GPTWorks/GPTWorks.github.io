========================================
PART 1 — THE BASICS (0:40–2:30)
========================================
[VISUAL] Hold up a short strand of end-glow fiber. Shine a flashlight into one end.
[YOU] "Fiber optics are thin strands that carry light from one end to the other.
      For a star ceiling we want end-glow fiber — light shoots out the tip like a star.
      Side-glow fiber lights along the length. That's for shooting-star lines, not dots."

[VISUAL] Show illuminator / LED light engine box.
[YOU] "The light engine is a small LED box. Fibers plug into it. Power stays low-voltage.
      Many kits include a twinkle wheel or remote so the stars flicker like a real sky."

[VISUAL] B-roll: bundle of fibers, remote, power cord.
[YOU] "You do not need glass fiber for a bedroom. Plastic end-glow fiber is easier, safer to cut,
      and made for home kits."

========================================
PART 2 — SAFETY (2:30–3:15)
========================================
[VISUAL] On-screen: SAFETY in bold. Point to outlet and engine.
[YOU] "Safety rules for this project:
      Use a purpose-built LED fiber illuminator — not a laser pointer.
      Keep the light engine in open air, off bedding, and away from curtains.
      If you rent, use adhesive mounts or ask before drilling.
      Turn power off before trimming fibers or opening the engine."

========================================
PART 3 — SHOPPING LIST (3:15–4:15)
========================================
[VISUAL] Lay parts on a table, name each one.
[YOU] "Here's what I use for a bedroom:
      — LED fiber optic illuminator with twinkle or remote
      — End-glow fiber strands, mixed thicknesses if you want bright and faint stars
      — Painter's tape and a constellation printout
      — A fine drill bit for drywall, or clear adhesive star mounts if you can't drill
      — Flush cutters or a sharp craft blade for clean fiber tips
      Optional: dimmer/remote batteries, and a short extension cord to hide the engine
        in a closet or behind a dresser."

========================================
PART 4 — PLAN THE SKY (4:15–5:20)
========================================
[VISUAL] Tape a paper map on the ceiling or hold it up to camera.
[YOU] "Decide the story of the sky. Mix a few real constellations
      with a soft random scatter so it still feels like wonder, not a textbook.

      Tip: fewer brighter stars look better than hundreds of dull ones.
      Mark each hole with a pencil dot. Leave a path for the fiber bundle to travel
      to the light engine — usually toward a corner or closet."

========================================
PART 5 — INSTALL (5:20–7:20)
========================================
[VISUAL] Drill or mount demo on scrap drywall if possible; otherwise show carefully on ceiling.
[YOU] "Step one: place the light engine where it can breathe and where the cord reaches an outlet.
      Step two: feed fibers from the ceiling points back to that corner. Keep bundles loose — no tight kinks.
      Step three: seat the fiber ends into the illuminator according to your kit.
      Step four: at the ceiling, pull each fiber until the tip is flush, then trim clean.
      A flat tip makes a crisp star. A ragged tip looks fuzzy.

      If you're using adhesive mounts instead of holes, stick the mount, press the fiber tip in,
      and hide the run along crown molding or behind a fabric canopy edge."

========================================
PART 6 — TWINKLE + FIRST LIGHT (7:20–8:20)
========================================
[VISUAL] Lights off. Turn engine on. Show remote twinkle modes.
[YOU] "Power on. Start dim. Then try twinkle.
      If one star is dead, check that fiber's seat in the engine and whether the tip is clean.
      If the whole sky is dim, the bundle may be bent too hard or not fully inserted.

      Patch paint around holes if needed. Hide the engine. Put the remote where you can reach it."
