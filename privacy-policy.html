Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This page explains how GPTWorks collects, uses, and protects information on this site.

Score My Landlord

  • Renters may submit reviews of past landlords, including a rental property address, South Carolina county, optional landlord name, experience answers, an optional comment, and an optional email.
  • We calculate a landlord score from the answers you provide.
  • Reviews are kept in our database. Greenville County, SC reviews may be posted publicly as we publish them. Reviews from other South Carolina counties are kept and will not be posted until that county has enough reviews to open.
  • We do not sell or share your information with anyone for any reason.
  • We do not contact landlords for or because of anyone’s review.

Other tools on this site

  • Some tools keep notes only in your browser (for example, study notes or research saves). Clearing site data removes those.
  • Where a tool uses a third-party service (such as a Custom GPT, Grok host, or X embed), that service’s own privacy terms also apply.
